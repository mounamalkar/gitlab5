Our complete process is:

1. Create repository
       ↓
2. Create a file
       ↓
3. Commit the file
       ↓
4. Create feature-branch
       ↓
5. Switch to feature-branch
       ↓
6. Make a change
       ↓
7. Commit the change
       ↓
8. Switch back to master
       ↓
9. Merge feature-branch into master
